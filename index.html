Why Raid With Us

Veteran Core

A new tag, not new raiders. Our core has years of Mythic progression together — and in the guilds that collapsed around us. We split off to build it right, and we know exactly which mistakes we're not repeating.

Standards From Pull One

Four focused nights with assignments posted before the first pull. Logs get reviewed between raids, prep is expected, and the standard is the same for officers and trials.

Ground Floor

Every kill under this tag is a first. Shape the roster and the culture instead of slotting into someone else's history. Progression matters — but so does wanting to log in.
